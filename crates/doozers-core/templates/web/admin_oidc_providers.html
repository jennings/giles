Identity providers

New identity provider

An identity provider is an OpenID Connect service, such as Google, Authentik, or Keycloak, that your users can sign in with. Every enabled provider is on the login page, and on the page an invite or recovery link leads to, where a user connects their account at it. Signing in with an account nobody connected is refused, so a provider never creates users.
